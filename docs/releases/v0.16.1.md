Glade 0.16.1 is a small patch release fixing a regression from 0.14: opening the menu bar popover turned Glade into a
background-only app until you quit, dropping it out of ⌘Tab and the Dock.

- **Glade stays in ⌘Tab and the Dock.** Since 0.14, opening the menu bar popover turned Glade into a background-only
  app until you quit: Electron flips the whole app's process type to a UIElement (accessory) app when the popover's
  panel window called `setVisibleOnAllWorkspaces(true, { visibleOnFullScreen: true })`. The window's own `panel` type
  already floats it above full-screen apps and on every Space, so the fix skips that process-type flip
  (`skipTransformProcessType: true`) and Glade keeps its normal ⌘Tab and Dock presence. (#365)

## Running it

- **Requirements:** a Mac with Apple silicon, logged in to [Claude Code](https://docs.claude.com/en/docs/claude-code).
- **Auth:** Glade runs on your own Claude Code login (or `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, if you have one set). It never asks for or
  stores your credentials.
- **Install:** download `Glade-0.16.1-arm64.dmg` below, open it and drag Glade to Applications. Your workspaces and
  tasks from 0.16.0 carry over.
- **First launch:** Glade isn't notarised by Apple yet, so the first time, right-click Glade in Applications and choose
  Open (on newer macOS, if there's no Open button, click Open Anyway in System Settings → Privacy & Security). If macOS
  still won't open it, run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Glade.app` and try again.
- **Logs:** `~/Library/Logs/glade/main.log`, one JSON line per event, rotated at 5 MB. They stay on your Mac;
  attach them to a bug report if you like (`docs/logs.md` says what's in them).
