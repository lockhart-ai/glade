Glade 0.4.0 is about questions: when the agent needs a decision from you, it asks, and waits for the answer.

## What's new

- **Questions from the agent.** When a choice is yours, the agent asks it on a card in the chat instead of guessing:
  options with a short description and a small sketch, pills to pick from, or a line of text. Its turn waits,
  however long you take, and carries on with your answers.
- **Answer with the mouse or the keyboard.** Click through the card, or Shift+Tab into it from the input bar: digits
  pick an option, the arrow keys move, Tab goes to the next question and Enter sends. Once sent, the card folds up to
  show what you chose.
- **Or just reply.** Typing a reply in the input bar while questions are open answers them in your own words. The card
  closes as "answered in your words", and your message goes to the agent as is.
- **Questions need you.** A task with open questions counts as Needs you. If you aren't looking at it, it's marked
  unread and a notification shows the first question.
- **Questions survive a restart.** If Glade quits while questions are open, they're still open when you open it again.
  Answering them picks the turn up where it left off.

Also in this release: tool calls cut off by a quit now show a neutral Interrupted row, and paused ones a purple
Paused row; notes in the tool log render `inline code`; and the right panel's tabs can be picked with ⌘⌥1–5, resized
by dragging its edge (kept across launches) and collapsed with ⌘⌥B.

## Running it

- **Requirements:** a Mac with Apple silicon, logged in to [Claude Code](https://docs.claude.com/en/docs/claude-code).
- **Auth:** Glade runs on your own Claude Code login (or `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, if you have one set). It never asks for or
  stores your credentials.
- **Install:** download `Glade-0.4.0-arm64.dmg` below, open it and drag Glade to Applications. Your tasks from 0.3.0
  carry over.
- **First launch:** Glade isn't notarised by Apple yet, so the first time, right-click Glade in Applications and choose
  Open (on newer macOS, if there's no Open button, click Open Anyway in System Settings → Privacy & Security). If macOS
  still won't open it, run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Glade.app` and try again.

Not in this release: the terminal and plugin panels, search, the contents of the Files, Todos, Artifacts and
Subagents tabs, and Settings. Those come in later phases.

## Calls made without Jared (please review)

- **Tested with a scripted agent only.** Every build has been exercised against Glade's scripted fake agent in the
  tests, not a real model deciding when to ask.
- **Names as drafted.** The tool is `ask`, with `choice`, `pills` and `text` questions, as `docs/model-surface.md`
  drafted them. Answers go back to the model keyed by question index, from 0.
- **Stricter questions than the draft.** Every text is trimmed and must not be empty, a choice or pills question needs
  at least two options, and option ids and pills must be unique. A question that doesn't fit is refused and nothing
  opens.
- **Every question is required** except a text question marked optional. Send answers stays disabled until they're
  all answered. The design's "2 of 3 answered" next to an enabled Send doesn't fit that.
- **Sketches are text.** An option's sketch is a few monospaced lines; lines starting with `#` are headings, shown in
  blue. The design draws abstract bars.
- **While questions are open** the task is waiting on you. A message you send then answers them in words; it isn't
  queued.
- **After a relaunch** open questions stay open rather than being asked again. Answering resumes the session and the
  turn carries on.
- **Claude Code's own AskUserQuestion tool is turned off,** so questions always come through `ask`.
- **The card doesn't take focus** when it opens; the input bar keeps it, and Shift+Tab reaches the card. Open question:
  should the card take focus, or get its own shortcut?
- **Questions notify and mark a task unread** like a reply does.
- **Interrupted and Paused tool rows** (P3 polish) show no progress numbers, which Glade can't know. A paused call
  becomes Interrupted once the task resumes.
- **Verification notes:** `03-rich-question` matches apart from sample data, the sketches, and Send answers being
  dimmed until every question is answered. Differences worth a look: the `ask` call shows in the tool log with its
  raw JSON input (the design has no row for it); after a relaunch the `ask` row reads Interrupted even though its
  questions stayed open and were answered; and mouse answering is covered by unit tests, but the e2e specs only drive
  the card by keyboard.
