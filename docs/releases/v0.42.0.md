v0.42.0 puts icons on the right panel's tabs and on the task list's Active and Done headers.

## What a user notices

- **Icons on the Todos, Agents and Files tabs (#777, PR #780).** Each tab has an icon ahead of its label and count: a checklist, the subagent glyph and a folder. When the right panel is narrow (about 380px or less), the labels drop and each tab shows its icon and count, with the label as its tooltip.
- **Icons on the Active and Done headers (#778, PR #779).** The task list's Active and Done sections get a play and a check beside their names, the same size and grey as Pinned's pin.

## Under the hood

- The tab bar's narrow layout is a CSS container query on the right panel, so it can't flicker as the panel resizes, and other tab bars in the app are unchanged (#780).
- Design screens 02, 08 and 50 show the tab icons, and 01 and 04 the section icons; the other screens keep the old look until they're next redrawn (#779, #780).

## Running it

- **Install:** download `Glade-0.42.0-arm64.dmg` below, open it and drag Glade to Applications. Your workspaces and tasks carry over.
- **Database:** no migrations in this release.
- **Signing:** the same self-signed certificate as before, so macOS permission grants carry over.
