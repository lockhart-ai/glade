Glade 0.13.0 is polish from setting Glade up on a second Mac, plus first-class **watchers**: see everything an agent
has left running or scheduled.

## New

- **Watchers.** When an agent watches something (a CI run, a PR, a queue, a remote job), it keeps writing its own
  script, and Glade shows it. A new **Watchers** tab (⌘⌥6) lists every Monitor watch, background command, scheduled
  wakeup and cron job the agent started, with its state, how many times it has woken the agent, its last output and
  when it's next due, and a **Stop** button. A task with live watchers shows an eye and a count in the sidebar.
- **Todo progress in the sidebar.** A task with todos shows a small ring and "3/7" on its row, and a check when
  they're all done.
- **Every agent reply is on a card.** Replies that don't need you sit on a neutral card; the one waiting on you keeps
  the purple one.
- **The working dots animate** (a calm pulse; still with Reduce motion on).
- **Styled scroll bars** everywhere, the terminal included, for Macs set to always show them.
- **The right panel's tabs scroll** when they don't fit, with chevrons at the ends.
- **The chat scrolls under the header card** instead of stopping short of it.
- **The workspace selector lines up** with the rest of the sidebar.

## Running it

- **Requirements:** a Mac with Apple silicon, logged in to [Claude Code](https://docs.claude.com/en/docs/claude-code).
- **Auth:** Glade runs on your own Claude Code login (or `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, if you have one set). It never asks for or
  stores your credentials.
- **Install:** download `Glade-0.13.0-arm64.dmg` below, open it and drag Glade to Applications. Your workspaces and
  tasks from 0.12.0 carry over.
- **First launch:** Glade isn't notarised by Apple yet, so the first time, right-click Glade in Applications and choose
  Open (on newer macOS, if there's no Open button, click Open Anyway in System Settings → Privacy & Security). If macOS
  still won't open it, run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Glade.app` and try again.
- **Logs:** `~/Library/Logs/glade/main.log`, one JSON line per event, rotated at 5 MB. They stay on your Mac;
  attach them to a bug report if you like (`docs/logs.md` says what's in them).

## Calls made without Jared (please review)

- The workspace badge and the collapse icon now sit on the sidebar's outer edge lines, flush with the search field and the + button. The sidebar's collapse button is a 24px square (it was 30px). (#253)
- Every agent reply now sits on a card. Replies that don't need you get a neutral card (the permission card's colours, in the question card's shape). The purple card stays for the reply that's waiting on you, so "needs you" still stands out. The narration above a question card is carded too. (#248)
- The working dots pulse in turn (opacity only, a 1.4s cycle, staggered 200ms), and hold still with Reduce motion on. (#249)
- The header floats over the chat, and the chat scrolls up under it. At the top of the scroll, the first message sits where it did before. Opening a search result counts a match hidden under the header as off screen, so it scrolls into view. (#252)
- When the right panel's tabs overflow, the strip scrolls with the wheel or trackpad, and 24px chevrons show at each end with more tabs past it. The selected tab always scrolls into view. The chevrons aren't in the Tab order, because the arrow keys already move through the tabs. (#251)
- A task row shows its todo progress as a small ring and "3/7", and "5/5" with a check when all are done. The tooltip names the item in progress. The progress is saved on the task, so the virtualised Done list needs no history, and it never reorders the sidebar. It hides on search results. (#246)
- Watchers are observed, not built by Glade (your direction). Monitor watches, background commands, scheduled wakeups and cron jobs that the agent starts are listed in a Watchers tab (⌘⌥6), showing state, wakes, last output and when each is next due, with a Stop button. The sidebar row shows an eye with the live count. No custom registration tool: the SDK's own mechanisms carry every script's wakes. (#250)
- Stopping a Monitor or command kills it. Stopping a wakeup or cron job blocks its future fires, because the SDK has no way to delete a job from outside the session; the agent isn't told, and the job lingers in its CronList until it expires or the session ends. (#250)
- After a relaunch, running watchers show "Stopped by the relaunch". Cron jobs show Suspended until their session resumes and lists them again. (#250)
- Background subagents stay in the Subagents tab and aren't listed as watchers. Six tabs overflow the default panel width, so Watchers can sit behind the right chevron until picked. (#250)
- The system prompt now asks the agent to run watch scripts with Monitor or run_in_background, not nohup or &. (#250)
- Every scroll bar is a thin rounded thumb (6px in a 10px bar) that lightens on hover and again while dragged; the terminal's matches. Worth knowing: on trackpad Macs the custom bars always show, instead of fading like macOS overlay scroll bars. The chat's thumb starts below the floating header. (#247)
